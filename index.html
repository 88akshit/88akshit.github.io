// 01 — About

Two disciplines,
one platform

My path ran through Infosys, Oracle, Cisco and VMware before I moved into battery-specific software at TWAICE, and then into leading platform development at BMW Group. That sequence matters — I came to batteries already knowing how to build resilient, scalable software, then spent years learning the electrochemistry itself.

Today I lead Cell Builder, a physics-based calculator for prismatic, cylindrical, and pouch cell design, and Material Explorer, a discovery tool for best-fit battery materials — both part of BMW's Battery Analytics Platform supporting next-generation cell engineering.

I also manage a team of interns across different workflows in battery cell research, and work daily across React, TypeScript, Java Spring Boot, Python, AWS, and LLM-driven RAG pipelines.

"Dive deep into solutions and improvise on architecture, code quality, for future-proof business needs and maintainability."
